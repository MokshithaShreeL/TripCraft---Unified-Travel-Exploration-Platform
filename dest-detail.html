Munnar — Kashmir of South India

Rolling emerald tea plantations, misty valleys, and the home of the endangered Nilgiri Tahr in the Western Ghats.

Overview & Experience

Situated at 1,600 metres above sea level, Munnar was once the summer resort of the British Government in South India. Today it is renowned for its vast expanse of tea gardens, colonial bungalows, aromatic spice trails, and pristine trekking routes like Anamudi (South India's highest peak).
