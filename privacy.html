Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Revised: March 1, 2026 · Compliant with DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

Data Controller: TripCraft Technologies Inc. Security: 256-Bit SSL + RFC 7519 JWT Contact: dpo@tripcraft.com

1. Information We Collect

At TripCraft, protecting your personal data and travel privacy is paramount. When you use our platform, we collect only necessary data points required to orchestrate your travel experiences:

  • Identity & Profile Data: Name, verified email address, contact telephone, and avatar initials.
  • Reservation & Itinerary Data: Selected travel dates, passenger counts (adults and children), origin and destination cities, flight/train selections, hotel preferences, and vehicle rental requirements.
  • Monetization & Photo Studio Content: High-resolution travel photos uploaded to your private photo books, custom captions, layout slot configurations, and royalty earnings balances.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Client-side storage keys, session duration, and recently viewed destinations.

2. Cryptographic Authentication & Local Storage

TripCraft employs an RFC 7519 JSON Web Token (JWT) architecture. Tokens are signed using cryptographic hash standards and encode user roles (ROLE_USER, ROLE_ADMIN) without exposing raw passwords.

To deliver an instant, zero-latency booking workflow, your active session, saved wishlist items, custom photo albums, and notification alerts are persisted client-side within browser localStorage and sessionStorage. You maintain full control over these local stores and can clear them at any time via browser settings or profile logout.

3. Payment Security & Financial Information

TripCraft utilizes 256-bit SSL encryption for all transaction communications. We adhere strictly to PCI-DSS standards:

  • Card numbers, CVV codes, and netbanking credentials are never stored in raw text on our servers or in client storage.
  • UPI payment requests route through certified Unified Payments Interface switches (NPCI).
  • Digital tax invoices and booking vouchers generated by our ledger reflect masked payment accounts for your privacy.

4. Solo Traveler Lounge & Community Privacy

Our dedicated Solo Community Hub and Buddy Matcher are built with proactive privacy safeguards:

  • Your exact phone number and home address are never made publicly visible in the Community Lounge.
  • Meetup proposals between solo travelers require mutual confirmation before travel dates and meeting points are shared.
  • Travelers can toggle their community profile between "Public Adventurer" and "Private" mode at any moment.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act of 2023 and European GDPR frameworks, you hold the following explicit rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can download a complete PDF dossier of all your active bookings, itinerary files, and photo albums at any time via My Bookings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may request complete purging of your booking history, profile credentials, and stored photobooks by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@tripcraft.com.
