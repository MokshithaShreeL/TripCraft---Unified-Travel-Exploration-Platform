Cancellation & Refund Policy

Clear, traveler-friendly refund timelines · Zero cancellation surcharge

Standard Cancellation Window: Up to 24-48 Hours Refund Processing: 3-5 Banking Days Wallet Payout: Instant

1. Transparent Cancellation Terms

At TripCraft, we believe travel planning should be stress-free and flexible. We clearly display the cancellation policy on every individual hotel, vehicle rental, and transport card before you confirm your booking.

Booking Category Cancellation Cutoff Refund Eligibility
Hotels & Mountain Resorts Up to 24 or 48 hours prior to check-in (12:00 PM) 100% Full Refund
Boutique Pods & Solo Hostels Up to 24 hours prior to check-in 100% Full Refund
Multi-Modal Rail (Vande Bharat) Up to 24 hours prior to train departure 90% Refund (as per IRCTC rules)
KSRTC Swift Sleeper Buses Up to 12 hours prior to scheduled boarding 85% Refund
Vehicle Rentals (Bikes & Cars) Up to 12 hours prior to dispatch time 100% Full Refund
Certified Local Guides Up to 12 hours prior to tour start 100% Full Refund

2. Settlement Methods & Timelines

When you cancel an eligible booking through your dashboard, you can choose between two refund destinations:

  • Instant TripCraft Wallet Credit: Credited immediately (0 seconds). Can be used across all flights, stays, vehicles, guides, or photo album purchases with no expiration date.
  • Original Payment Method (UPI / Bank / Card): Settled within 3 to 5 banking business days through our payment gateway switch. Confirmation SMS and email receipt are dispatched immediately upon initiation.

3. Cross-Booking Relational Adjustments

TripCraft's smart relational architecture connects secondary rentals (e.g. car or bike rentals linked via hotel_booking_id) to your primary hotel stay. If you cancel or modify your hotel booking, TripCraft will proactively prompt you to cancel or reschedule your vehicle rental with zero penalty fees.

4. Weather Disruptions & Force Majeure

In the event of unforeseen natural conditions—such as heavy monsoon landslides in Munnar, snowfall pass closures in Manali/Ladakh, or airline groundings—TripCraft automatically overrides standard cutoffs to issue 100% full refunds or complimentary date rescheduling upon receipt of local authority advisories.

5. How to Initiate a Cancellation

You can cancel any eligible booking in three simple clicks:

  1. Navigate to My Bookings from the top navigation dropdown.
  2. Locate the reservation card and click "Cancel Reservation".
  3. Review the refund amount breakdown and select your preferred settlement method (Original Account or Wallet).
