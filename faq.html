Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking verification, dynamic seasonal rates, free cancellation policies, and automated tax invoices.

All listings displaying our Free Cancellation badge can be cancelled up to 24 or 48 hours prior to arrival (clearly stated on the listing and invoice) for a 100% full refund with zero cancellation penalties.

Refunds are processed automatically through our gateway ledger back to your original source of payment (UPI, Credit/Debit card, or Net Banking) within 3 to 5 business days. You can initiate cancellation with a single click from your My Bookings dashboard.

TripCraft reflects realistic real-world travel market dynamics. Prices automatically calibrate depending on your selected travel dates:

  • Standard Weekdays (Mon-Thu): Standard rack rate multiplier (1.0x).
  • Weekend Surge (Fri-Sun): +15% demand rate for high-occupancy mountain and coastal getaways.
  • Peak Holiday Months (Oct-Jan, May): +20% peak season rate reflecting festival and summer holidays.

You can clearly see the dynamic tag and price breakdown inside the reservation modal as soon as you select your check-in date.

Immediately upon completing payment, a unique Booking Reference Number (e.g., #TC-BK-2026-89421) is issued. You receive an on-screen official Tax Invoice with complete GST breakdown, hotel check-in timings, and inclusions.

You can download an official PDF copy at any time by clicking "Download PDF Invoice" in the modal or navigating to My Bookings.
