Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common queries from travelers embarking on solo journeys.

How safe is solo female travel across the featured destinations?
All destinations featured on TripCraft undergo rigorous safety indexing (rated 1 to 10) combining official tourism data, verified traveler reports, and local precinct records. Destinations like Munnar (8.8) and Andaman (9.2) maintain exceptionally low crime rates, certified female-friendly homestays, and active tourist police desks.
How does the one-tap Emergency SOS dispatch function?
When you tap the SOS button in your safety dashboard, our system instantly broadcasts your real-time encrypted GPS coordinates to your registered emergency contacts via SMS and WhatsApp, while concurrently pinging our 24/7 emergency response coordination center.
Can I connect with other solo travelers without sharing my personal details?
Yes! The TripCraft Community and Travel Buddy platform uses verified masked profiles. You can chat, coordinate shared transportation, and join group departures entirely within the platform before deciding to share any personal contacts.
What accommodations are recommended for solo explorers?
We curate verified social backpacker hostels (like Zostel), boutique female residences, and family-run hill cottages equipped with 24/7 reception, keycard lockers, safe luggage storage, and active communal areas for meeting fellow travelers.
What cancellation policy applies if my solo travel plans change?
Every verified solo stay and tour package booked on TripCraft features free cancellation up to 48 hours before scheduled arrival with 100% instant refund to your original payment method directly tracked on your "My Bookings" page.
